Overview

Welcome to the Brigham Young University Algebraic Geometry group's website. We are a group of faculty and students working on classical and modern problems in algebraic geometry — real and complex geometry, enumerative geometry, computational experiments, and interactions with topology.

This site hosts our seminar schedule, links to notes, and resources for students. See the seminar page for the Winter 2026 schedule.
